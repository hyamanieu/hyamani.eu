## About my expertise

### My assessment

Modern laboratory and industrial environments generate large volumes of data from production equipment, legacy and modern industrial protocols, business applications, quality systems, and operational processes. The challenge is not collecting data, but turning it into reliable information that supports daily operations, performance monitoring, and strategic decision making.

### My work

I design, build, and operate industrial data platforms that connect production systems with business intelligence and analytics tools. My expertise covers the full data lifecycle, including:

- Industrial data acquisition and integration (MQTT, OPC-UA, Modbus, custom interfaces, APIs)
- Data pipeline design and orchestration
- ETL/ELT workflows and data transformation
- Relational and time-series data management
- Data marts and KPI-oriented analytical models
- Production performance and productivity reporting
- Containerized application deployment and operation
- Identity and access management for data-driven applications
- Dashboarding and reporting platforms (Power BI, Grafana, custom applications)
- Data governance and single-source-of-truth architectures

### Platform Independence

I design solutions to minimize vendor lock-in and keep switching costs low. From hosting infrastructure to AI models and LLM providers, my architectures prioritize portability, interoperability, and the freedom to change suppliers at little cost.

{{< details summary="Technical details & technologies" >}}

**Infrastructure and hosting**

I design portable solutions for deployment across a wide range of environments:

- **Cloud providers:** Microsoft Azure, AWS, Google Cloud, OVHcloud, Hetzner, and other providers.
- **On-premise infrastructure:** Physical servers, virtual machines, and hypervisors.
- **Deployment and orchestration:** Docker, Terraform, and SkyPilot, selected according to the project's needs.

The goal is to make infrastructure replaceable without rebuilding the entire solution.

**AI and LLM integration**

I help organizations integrate AI capabilities without becoming dependent on a single model or supplier:

- **Commercial LLM APIs:** Integration with leading providers, including OpenAI, Anthropic, Mistral, and Google.
- **Open-weight models:** Deployment of models from Hugging Face and other model ecosystems, on-premise or on remote infrastructure.
- **Independent inference:** Self-hosted inference environments for greater control over data, deployment, and operating costs.
- **LLM routing and cost optimization:** Tools such as LiteLLM to centralize access to multiple providers and simplify model switching.

{{< /details >}}

**My principle:** Choose the best-performing solution for the use case, retain control over the architecture, and keep the cost of switching providers as low as reasonably possible.


### My Approach

My approach combines data engineering, software engineering, and industrial operations knowledge to deliver pragmatic, maintainable, and business-focused solutions. Whether the objective is production monitoring, operational excellence, traceability, quality management, or executive reporting, I help organizations create robust data platforms that provide trusted information where and when it is needed.


I firmly believe in sovereign architecture based on open source software.
