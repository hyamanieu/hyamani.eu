Many organizations recognize the potential of data and artificial intelligence but lack a clear understanding of where to start, which use cases to prioritize, or what foundations are required to succeed.
<!--more-->

There are two main objectives provided by this service:

- identify the primary pain point
- identify quick wins

This service provides a structured assessment of the current situation, including operational processes, production systems, data availability, reporting practices, and existing technology platforms.

The objective is to identify opportunities that can generate measurable business value while remaining technically and operationally feasible.

Typical deliverables include:
- Inventory of available data sources and systems
- Evaluation of data quality, accessibility, and governance
- Identification of additional operational and business pain points
- Prioritization of data and AI initiatives based on value and complexity
- Definition of key performance indicators and decision-support requirements
- High-level architecture recommendations
- Implementation roadmap and project sequencing

This service is intended for executive teams, operations leaders, digital transformation programs, and organizations seeking a practical starting point for data-driven initiatives in the industry.
